# A Matter of Time — v3.2 update

Apply this to the existing v3.1 build. **Where this file and `CLAUDE.md` disagree, this file wins.** When you're done, rewrite `CLAUDE.md` to describe v3.2.

## 1. New `content/story.md`

Replace `content/story.md` with the attached version. Every line of player-facing text has been rewritten in a New York Times register, with American spelling and no colons, semicolons or dashes used as punctuation. Effects and the card map are unchanged, apart from the format changes below.

### Format changes the parser must handle

- **New field on every Scenario and Event: `**Timeline label:**`**, a two-to-five-word summary such as "Istanbul earthquake" or "AI best friends in Japan".
- **Future stories now have their own fields.** Each `### Story N` contains:
  - `**Image:**` (empty for now)
  - `**Image description:**`
  - `**Text:**` (multi-paragraph; the story text)

  The Future-level `**Image:**` and `**Image description:**` fields are gone. **The card shown at the Future reveal uses the image of the story that was picked.** Until images exist, use the placeholder front.
- **Futures renamed** (ids unchanged): `partnership` → Common Ground, `upload` → Afterlife, `escape` → Elsewhere, `gilded_few` → Gilded, `last_war` → Last Light. The Garden, Blackout, The Oracle and The Long Middle keep their names. Read titles from the file and never hard-code them.
- **Id rename:** `r2-datacentre` → `r2-datacenter`.
- **Value colors are new and muted** to match the tarot cards. Read them from the file.

### Style checks in `tools/check-story.js`

Add a lint pass over **player-facing text only**: headlines, timeline labels, stories, questions, choice text, results, lead-ins, Future titles, taglines and story text, Value text, UI text and history lines. Exclude image descriptions, the Intro (it's Levi's own wording), and the notes at the top of the file. Report each hit with the card id and the field:

- **Punctuation:** any `;`, `—` (em dash), `–` (en dash), a spaced hyphen ` - `, or `:`. Allow a colon between two digits, as in times like 2:14 a.m.

## 2. Bug: glitch when "As a result…" appears

After a Choice is picked, there's a visible jump as the other two Choices fade and the result panel comes in. Reproduce it, then fix it so the transition is smooth:

- Don't remove the unchosen Choices from layout (`display: none`) until their fade has fully finished. Wait for `transitionend` or use a timer matched to the fade.
- Keep the Choices container at a fixed height while they fade, so nothing below it shifts.
- Then animate the chosen card into its final position using a transform (the FLIP technique), and fade the result panel in on top, positioned so its arrival causes no reflow of anything above it.
- Animate only `opacity` and `transform`. Make sure the click handler can't fire twice and nothing re-renders mid-transition.

## 3. Timeline strip during play

- **Every entry uses its `Timeline label`**, never the full headline, and is never truncated with an ellipsis. Labels are short; allow them to wrap to two lines if needed.
- **Events show their own label** (for example "Istanbul earthquake") in the grey Event style, not the words "Out of your hands". Keep the grey card styling, which works well.
- **Move the strip down** for breathing room. It currently crowds the top of the screen. Give it about 32–40px of space above the tops of the labels (more on phones with a notch, using `env(safe-area-inset-top)`).

## 4. Flow fixes

### Intro skip

The **Skip** control in the bottom-right corner of the Intro becomes a **white pill**: off-white background (around `#ece6da`), dark text, about 14px, padding of about 8px by 18px, `border-radius: 999px`, with a soft shadow and a slight brighten on hover. It should be visible from the first moment of the Intro.

### From the history Timeline into the game (redesigned)

- The history Timeline's vertical line **ends at the Now marker**. It must not extend below Now.
- Once Now is reached, **pause for about 1.2 s** (shorter than v3.1).
- Then, with no screen change or cut, **keep scrolling smoothly down past Now into darkness** (about 1.2 s, easing out), so the history slides up and away.
- As it settles, the **Timeline strip fades in at the top**, the **deck slides in**, and the **first card is dealt**. The game begins on the same continuous surface.

### Play again

**Play again** skips the Intro, the Disclaimer and the history Timeline animation entirely. It goes straight to the game-start transition: strip fades in, deck slides in, first card is dealt.

## 5. "See what you chose"

- **The timeline ends behind the final Future card.** Its spine should stop at the center of the Future card, tucked behind it, and must not continue past 2070 or below the card.
- Value **pills and bars use the new muted colors** from story.md.

## 6. Text colors and contrast

Some text is too dark to read, and some is too close to the darkest shade text should ever be. Define text color tokens and use them everywhere:

- **Primary text:** about `#ece6da`
- **Secondary text:** about `#c9c2b5`. Use this for the end labels under each Value bar ("Useful tools", "Beyond us"), datelines, the timeline labels and small captions. Nothing should be darker than this for readable text.
- **Faint, decorative only** (never for words someone needs to read): about `#8f897e`
- **Accent-colored text** (Future taglines, Value pill text) must meet at least 4.5:1 contrast against the background. At runtime, if an accent color falls short, lighten it toward the primary text color until it passes. This fixes "Still arguing. Still deciding." and any similar cases.

## 7. Particles

Increase again to about **350 on desktop** and **160 on phones**, keeping the two depth layers, the gentle cursor push and the reduced-motion support. Check that the frame rate stays smooth, using one canvas and no per-particle DOM elements.
