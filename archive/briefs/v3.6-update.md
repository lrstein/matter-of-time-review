## 1. Automated play-testing (priority)

### Test hooks in the game

- **`?seed=N`** makes every random choice in the engine reproducible (card draws, Events, story picks). Use one seeded random number generator everywhere, and never call `Math.random()` directly.
- **`?test=1`** turns on test mode:
  - Intro and Disclaimer are skipped and audio is muted.
  - Animations run at about 10 times speed, not instantly, so ordering and layout bugs still show.
  - A read-only `window.__game` object exposes the engine state. It should include:
    - the current round
    - the Values
    - the expected timeline items, each with type (scenario, event or milestone), id, date, year and label, in order
    - the gauge level
    - the chosen Future and story
- Test mode must never be active without the URL parameter.

### `tools/play-test.js` (Playwright)

The harness plays complete games in a real browser. Install with `npm i -D playwright` and `npx playwright install chromium`, and run with `node tools/play-test.js --games 300`.

**What each test run covers:**
- Many seeds, with random choices each game.
- Both a desktop viewport (1440 × 900) and a phone viewport (390 × 844).
- Some games that open "Read the full story" and "Read more", and some that don't.
- Some games that scroll the timeline back and forth mid-game.
- Sequences of **three Play again replays in a row** without reloading.
- Rapid double-clicks on buttons.

**After every transition and at the end of each game, check:**

- **Chronology.** Markers on the timeline strip are in date order from left to right (x positions never decrease as dates increase). Markers on the results timeline are in date order from top to bottom. A marker never appears and then moves backward.
- **Completeness.** The number and identity of markers matches `window.__game`'s expected timeline exactly, with no missing, duplicated or extra markers. This includes the stray-marker bug fixed in v3.3.
- **Labels.** Every marker has a non-empty, visible label (not clipped, not zero opacity). Label boxes don't overlap one another beyond a small tolerance.
- **Types.** Scenario markers use the scenario icon (`#eacc9d` tint), Event markers the Event icon (`#85837f`), and milestones the diamond (`#9fb4c7`). **A milestone is never rendered as a card.**
- **Gauge.** The gauge level on screen matches the engine.
- **Reveal.** The reveal shows the title, tagline, the "How we got here" sentences and the story. The top strip hides as the Future card rises.
- **Results timeline.** It ends behind the Future card and is in date order. The Value bars match the engine's Values.
- **Replays.** Play again starts cleanly, with no leftover markers, panels, listeners or audio from the previous game. Unseen cards are preferred, as in v3.4.
- **Health.** There are no console errors or warnings, no unhandled promise rejections and no element stuck invisible that should be visible. Every Continue and Choice button can fire only once.

**On failure:** save a screenshot, the seed, the viewport and the step, so any failure can be reproduced exactly with `?seed=N&test=1`.

**Pass bar:** at least 1,000 games across both viewports, including replay sequences, with **zero failures**.

### `tools/check-story.js` additions

- **Chronology check across every possible link.** A card's date must be no later than any card it links to. An Event's date must be later than the card it follows and no later than every card in its Next list. My own check of the attached story.md found no violations.
- Keep the 100,000-game simulation. It should report zero ordering violations and the number of cards and Events per game (currently 4 to 6).

## 2. Known bugs and root causes

- **Markers added out of order, then backtracking.** In v3.5, milestones were shown by round number ("shown after round 2") instead of by date, and the Event was always placed before milestones. So "Intelligence gets cheap" (2034) could appear after round 2 even when the next card was dated 2032 or 2033, and "Robots catch up" (2036) could appear after a 2037 Event. **Fix:** at each transition, gather the Event (if any) and every milestone dated after the previous card and on or before the next card. Sort them all by date, with ties going to the Event first, and show them in that order. In my simulation of 100,000 games with this rule, the timeline was in chronological order every time.
- **Smooth insertion.** When a marker is added, compute the final layout of the whole strip first, then animate the new marker into its slot while existing markers ease to any new positions. Never insert a marker and then visibly re-sort.
- **Markers without labels.** Every marker renders its `Timeline label`. If space is tight, let labels wrap or stack, but never drop them.
- **Milestones shown as cards.** In v3.6, milestones are never cards (see §4).

## 3. Scenarios show a summary first

- Every Scenario and Event in story.md now has a **`**Summary:**`** field, two or three sentences long and enough to make a decision on.
- A scenario card shows the dateline, headline and **summary**, followed by a quiet **"Read the full story"** toggle that expands the full Story below the summary, with a smooth height and fade animation. It is collapsed by default on every new card.
- The question and the Choices always remain visible below.
- "As a result…" is unchanged.

## 4. Out of your hands: a panel between rounds, not a card

- Events are **no longer drawn from the deck or flipped.** After the "As a result…" beat, an Event appears as a lighter interstitial panel in the grey Event style. The panel shows:
  - the "Out of your hands" label
  - the date and place
  - the headline
  - the **summary**, with a **"Read more"** toggle for the full story
  - a **Continue** button
- After Continue, its marker joins the timeline strip at its date, labeled with its `Timeline label` (for example "Istanbul earthquake").
- Frequency is unchanged: a chance after round 2 and after round 3, so at most two per game.

## 5. State of the Art becomes a capability gauge

State of the Art items are now **milestones**, never cards.

### The gauge

- It's a small, quiet element (top right below the mute button, or another unobtrusive spot) with four marks: **Tools → Expert → AGI → Superintelligence**. It starts at **Expert** in 2026.
- When a milestone appears during a transition:
  - Its diamond marker (`#9fb4c7`) joins the timeline strip at its date.
  - A one-line caption appears near the gauge for about 4 seconds, for example "2031 · AGI achieved".
  - If the milestone's `Gauge` is a level name, the gauge fills smoothly to that mark with a soft glow. If it's `hold`, the gauge gives a gentle pulse.
- **Hovering** the gauge, a diamond or the caption (or tapping on phones) shows the milestone's `Short definition`.
- **Milestones never stop play.** No click is needed. They animate in during the transition, about 1.5 s each.

### Placement

- Use the date-ordered interleaving in §2.
- `Condition` and `Slot` work as in v3.5. For example, AGI appears if Power is at least 0, and otherwise "Progress steadies" appears.
- Effects apply as each milestone appears.

### Results screen

Add a collapsible **State of the art** section listing each milestone the player reached, with its full **What happened**, **What it means** and **How life changes** text.
