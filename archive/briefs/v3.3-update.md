# A Matter of Time — v3.3 update

Apply this to the existing v3.2 build. **Where this file and `CLAUDE.md` disagree, this file wins.** When you're done, rewrite `CLAUDE.md` to describe v3.3.

The overall feel for v3.3 is **a slow, zen pace**. Nothing pops in. Everything fades in and out gently.

## 1. New `content/story.md`

Replace `content/story.md` with the attached version. Content changes, for reference:

- **Logic pass.** Lead-ins and results that relied on forced or unconvincing reasoning were rewritten or relinked.
- **Two Future stories rewritten.** The Garden's dancer story now takes place in Dakar, and The Oracle's "Thursday people" story is now grounded in a real decision, with all technology left in a basket at the door.
- **Odd phrasing removed.**
- **Source links.** Every reference to a real past event or statistic now links to a news source: the 2017 Finland experiment, the East India Company, the 2026 Hugging Face breach, Standard Oil and others.
- **Requirements.** Futures have new `**Requires:**` rules (see §2), and The Oracle's Control target is now slightly toward Adrift.

### Parser changes

1. **Links inside text.** Any player-facing text may contain Markdown links: `[visible words](https://url)`.
   - Render them as real links that open in a new tab (`target="_blank" rel="noopener"`).
   - Style them subtly, with a thin dotted underline in the secondary text color that shifts to the current accent color on hover. They should invite a click without breaking the reading flow.
   - Links can appear in Scenario stories, results, Future stories, and Value "Why it matters" text.
2. **`**Requires:**` on Futures,** for example `handover >= 3, unity >= 1, control <= 0`. Operators are `>=` and `<=`, and Value names are the usual six.
3. **New SETTINGS:**
   - `**Round colors:**`, four hex colors for rounds 1 to 4.
   - `**Event color:**` for Out of your hands cards.
4. **Optional `**Weight:**`** on any Scenario or Event (default 1). It multiplies that card's Effects. No card uses it yet, but support it so individual decisions can later count for more or less.

### `tools/check-story.js` changes

- **Lint:** strip Markdown links (keeping their visible words) before the punctuation and spelling checks, so URLs don't trigger colon warnings. Flag any link that isn't `https://`, and any link to Wikipedia.
- **Alignment report:** after the simulation, print the average final Values for players who reached each Future, and confirm that no Future was ever given to a player who fails its Requires. Expected averages are roughly:
  - **The Garden:** Control +2.4, Equality +1.8, Reality −1.3
  - **Common Ground:** Unity +1.8, Power +1.4
  - **Afterlife:** Reality +3.0, Control +2.1
  - **Elsewhere:** Reality +1.8, Equality −1.0
  - **Gilded:** Equality −3.2, Control +2.6
  - **Last Light:** Unity −2.1, Control −1.0
  - **Blackout:** Control −2.1, Reality +2.3
  - **The Oracle:** Handover +4.5, Unity +2.3, Control −1.4

## 2. Choosing the Future (changed)

The results screen must always agree with the ending, so Futures now have hard requirements as well as targets:

1. Rank all Futures (except The Long Middle) by distance to the player's final Values, closest first.
2. Go down the list. The player receives the first Future that is within the Long Middle threshold **and** meets every condition in its `Requires`.
3. If none qualifies, the player receives The Long Middle.

The threshold is now **2.6** (read from SETTINGS). Expected shares with random choices:

- The Garden ~13%
- Common Ground ~12%
- Afterlife ~4%
- Elsewhere ~7%
- Gilded ~8%
- Last Light ~8%
- Blackout ~4%
- The Oracle ~13%
- The Long Middle ~30%

Every card still appears in some games.

## 3. Round colors

- **Each round has its own accent color** (from `Round colors`). Events use `Event color`.
- The round's color is used for that card's glow, the question highlight, the Choice hover borders, the "As a result…" label, and the transition into the next card.
- When a card is placed on the timeline strip, **the year label for that marker is shown in that round's color**, so the strip becomes a row of colored years.
- **On the Future reveal and in "See what you chose,"** the whole recap timeline, including every marker, year and connector, slowly recolors (about 1.5 s fade) to **the Future's accent color**, as if the ending had colored everything that led to it.

## 4. "As a result…"

Make it bigger and give it more presence:

- The "As a result…" label is larger, in the display font at about 1.5 times the body size.
- The result text is about 22–24px on desktop (20px on phones), comfortably wider, and keeps the sentence-by-sentence fade but slower (see §8).

## 5. Timeline strip (during play)

- **Chronological scale.** Place markers on one continuous linear time scale, using the same years-per-pixel for the history section and for the player's future. For example, run it from 2017 to 2050 with Now at 2026, so the gaps between markers reflect real time.
  - Convert dates as follows: an exact year stays as it is, "Late 2030s" becomes 2038, and "2040s" becomes 2045.
  - When two markers would overlap, alternate them slightly above and below the line instead of letting them collide.
  - Subtle tick marks every five years are fine.
- **Seamless card-to-strip animation.** The card should shrink along a gentle curved path (about 1.4 s, ease-in-out) to the **exact position** of its new marker, crossfading into the marker's thumbnail as it arrives. The marker must not appear before the card lands, and nothing should jump.
- **Remove the small stray marker in the middle of the strip.** Find whatever decorative element or empty entry is drawing it. Every marker on the strip must belong to a real history event, Now, a player decision or an Event.

## 6. Dates

Dates must read exactly as written in story.md, for example "2040s" with a lowercase s. Remove any `text-transform: uppercase` or small-caps styling from dates and datelines. If small caps are wanted for style, apply them to the place name only.

## 7. Skip button

- Move it further from the corner, about 40px from the bottom and right edges (respecting safe areas on phones).
- Make it larger: about 16px text and about 11px by 24px of padding.
- **Less stark.** Use an off-white background at about 55% opacity (for example `rgba(236, 230, 218, 0.55)`) with dark text. The whole pill sits at about 70% opacity until hovered, then eases to fully opaque over about 400ms.

## 8. Pace, fades and gradients

- **Slow everything down a little.** Screen crossfades take about 900–1,100ms, text reveals about 700ms each with about 250ms between staggered items, and use a soft ease such as `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`. Nothing should appear or disappear instantly, so every element fades in and out.
- **Longer gradients.** Any fade-out mask or glow (the fading history above Now on the results screen, card glows, edge fades) should transition over a longer distance, about 30–40% of the element instead of 10–15%, so things dissolve more slowly.

## 9. Sound

- **Click:** the current click is still too harsh. Make it a soft, low, felted tap: a sine around 300 Hz gliding to 220 Hz over about 80ms, an 8ms attack, about 180ms decay, peak gain around 0.015, and a low-pass at about 1,200 Hz. It should feel like a fingertip on wood rather than a beep.
- **Card flip swoosh:** softer and mellower. Use a low-pass-filtered noise sweep of about 250 → 900 → 400 Hz, roughly 250ms attack and 500ms release, peak gain around 0.025. Keep it timed to the midpoint of the visual flip.
- **Choice confirmation** (the two-note rise): make it quieter and slower, with notes about 140ms apart.
- The ambient music stays as it is.
