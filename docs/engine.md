# site/js/ — engine, UI and test hooks

Read this before editing `site/js/` (it was `js/CLAUDE.md` until the launch layout). The project overview and terminology are in the root `CLAUDE.md`; content rules are in `docs/content.md`.

## Engine (`site/js/engine.js`)

### Values, scaling and Futures

All Values start at 0; each Choice, Event and milestone adds its Effects. **Every test of the Values uses the scaled Values**: the raw Values × min(1, `Value scale rounds` ÷ rounds played). After 6 rounds that's 5/6. **Round influence** multiplies each Choice's Effects by its round's entry (0.75 in round 1 up to 1.5 in rounds 5 and 6), so later decisions move the world more and the stakes rise; Events and milestones are not multiplied. The results screen's bars show the scaled Values. **Lost values (v5.6):** once the world has tipped over, `counted()` drops Effects on SETTINGS → Values lost at the tipping point (Choices, milestones and era drift), and `state.snapshot` holds the scaled Values from the moment it tipped, which the results show for those four. Each Choice's log entry records `applied` (each Value's change × Round influence) and `lost` (the Values it would have moved that no longer count).

- `distance(values, future)`: root-mean-square difference over only the Values that Future's Targets define.
- `meets(state, future)`: whether the game meets every Requires term (Values scaled, flags over the whole game).
- `grounding(state, future)`: the Future's Grounded by entries that happened, in order. A Choice matches `card/Letter`, an Event or tipping beat its id.
- `lookingBack(matches, future, state)`: up to three sentences (v5.2). If the Future requires flags, the decisions that set them come first (the engine records what first set every flag in `state.source`); then the latest match; then the most recent matches from rounds not yet represented; the earliest only if nothing else is left. In the order they happened. A Grounded by sentence may use `{year}` (the match's date).
- `pull(state, future)`: its grounding matches, each counting `Future pull decay` ^ (rounds before the last). `wellGrounded(matches, distance)`: at least `Future min grounding` matches with one from round `Future recent round` on, or one match from the last two rounds, or one recent match within `Future close distance` of the Targets.
- `leaders(state)`: the Futures the game is heading for, by distance − `Future pull` × pull (+1 if a Value Requires isn't met yet, +3 a point beyond the Long Middle threshold), leaving out Futures whose required flags aren't set and, before a tipping point, those that need one.
- `pulledApart(state)` (v5.3): The Long Middle's How we got here, the latest decision toward each of the two leading Futures, then UI TEXT → Long Middle looking back.
- `chooseFuture(state)`: if the world tipped over, a Future that requires `after_humanity` (Overgrowth) if the game meets it and it's grounded, otherwise the beat's Future, whatever the distance; The Long Middle never follows a tipping point. Otherwise, of the Futures within `Long Middle threshold` that meet their Requires and are well grounded, the one with the lowest distance − `Future pull` × pull; if none, The Long Middle.

### Picking cards (storylets)

At the start of each round (after the round step, below):

1. **The pool**: every Scenario whose Round includes this round, dated no more than `Most years between cards` (9 since v5.5) after the last card (v5.3, `state.cardYear`), whose Appears when holds (capability and reached terms tested against the world **at the card's own date**: `capabilityAt(state, year)` works out the pending milestones up to that year exactly as the transition will show them, so the card and the milestones before it always agree), unseen this game, not dated before the last card or Event, and (after a tipping point) marked After humanity, or (before one) not.
2. **Session novelty** (replays): if any of the most continuous cards (affinity at least 3/4 of the top) is new this session, cards already seen this session drop out (`fresh` lists the rest, for the harness).
3. **Score**: Weight × `Continuity bonus` ^ affinity × `Spine bonus` ^ spine × `Scale penalty` if the card's Scale is below the round's (Round scales) × the round's `Steering bonus` (v5.2: one per round, `1, 1, 1, 4, 12, 80`) from `Steering from round` if the card continues the story (affinity above 0) and a Choice on it grounds the leader (× the fourth root more if another Choice grounds the runner-up; × the square root if it grounds only the runner-up) × `Agents bonus` for agents cards (those that require `agents_era`) during the agents era (and `Agents penalty` from round 4 for cards that don't mention `agents_era`) × `Novelty bonus` if unseen. **Affinity** adds up, over each Follows entry the game has set, its strength × `Continuity decay` ^ age (age = rounds since the decision; an Event counts as half a round before the next card), so every earlier decision counts and the last counts most (`state.when` records when each flag was set; content without Follows falls back to the flags its conditions and openers mention). **Spine** (`onSpine`) is the weight of the card's strongest storyline among the player's: each storyline adds `Spine decay` ^ age for every card chosen on it, and the strongest is scaled to 1 (`state.threads`; `state.spine` names it).
4. **Draw**: before `Best-fit from round` (always, at 7), in proportion to score. From then on, the best card (ties broken by the explore score, or in the last round the tiebreaker score, then file order) with `Best-fit pick chance`, otherwise one of the rest in proportion to score.
5. **Answers** (v5.4): after an Event with Demands response, the pool is first narrowed to the cards that answer it (one of their openers or conditions names it), if any qualify. (v6.0) A Choice can demand a response too (`demandingChoice()`): the next card answers it the same way, and no Event is planned in between (`eventPool()` is empty).
5a. **The pace question** (v5.5): in round `Pace question by round` (3), if none of the `Pace cards` has been drawn this game, the pool (after any answers) narrows to the Pace cards that qualify, if any do.
5b. **The push for more AI** (v5.9): from round `Pressure question by round` (5), if the world is held or leveled now (`slowWorld()`, from `paceOf`), no Event just demanded a response, there is no tipping point and none of the `Pressure cards` has been drawn, the pool narrows to the Pressure cards that qualify, if any do.
6. **Safety net** if the pool is ever empty: any unused card of the round, conditions ignored, in date order; then any unused card of the round. `check-story.js` counts every use (currently none).
7. **The opener**: of the card's openers whose condition holds, the one tied to the most recently set flag (ties to the first listed); openers on named flags with no age come after those; else its default. `pickLine` does this for openers and milestones' Because lines (v6.0: `pickLineAt` also returns `cite`, the set flags its line names, which a shown milestone's log entry keeps so `check-story.js` can report two milestones in a row explained by the same decision), and fills `{year}` with the year the youngest flag its condition names was set (`state.years`, recorded by `setFlag`).

### Choices, flags and the arcs

`choose(state, card, choice)` applies the Effects (times Round influence), sets the Choice's own flag, the card's id and its Sets (counting each), ends any flags it Clears, adds Agents and Brake to the balance (`brake()`: a positive brake on a world-scale card, or a Choice that sets `pause` or `restraint`, also sets `brake_world`; an Event's Brake works the same way in `applyEvent`; racing Choices carry negative brakes), and records the decision. Every Choice also sets the card's Threads, and an Event sets its own. `Mostly flags` (`tends_life tended_life`): after a tipping point, `tended_life` is set while at least half of the After humanity Choices made set `tends_life`, and cleared if that stops being true. Then its **tipping point**, if it has one and the world hasn't tipped: checked with the scaled Values after the Choice. If it holds, the beat for its type happens (`after_humanity` and the beat's id are set, and a tipping entry is logged at the card's date); `choose` returns the beat. (v5.6) The entry also carries `text` (the first `### Version` whose When holds, else the beat's Text), `why` (the Choice's Tipping why, `{year}` filled) and `prevented` (the beat's Prevented line picked like an opener, leaving out lines that name the tipping card itself), and `state.snapshot`. If every Value term is within 1 point of holding but not over, `near_miss` and `near_<type>` are set (once), `Near miss effects` apply, and its Near miss Event follows (v5.4).

### Transitions: Events, the round step and milestones

`planTransition(state)` draws, in one seeded draw from `transitionOdds(state)`, everything up to the next card:

1. **The Event.** Its likelihood × `Event on-spine factor` (2.5) if one of its threads weighs at least half the player's spine, otherwise × `Event off-spine factor` (0.35) (v5.3). Eligible: its Round includes the round just played, Appears when holds, unseen this game, not dated before the last card, no tipping point yet, fewer than `Most Events per game`, and no Event in the previous transition. Replays prefer Events unseen this session. Each eligible Event rolls its Likelihood; if several succeed, the likeliest is shown (exact odds are computed for the seeded draw). Events without a Likelihood use the v4.1 flat chance.
2. **The round step** (`{ kind: "round" }`): era drift for the new round, and `agents_era` (from `Agents from round`, once the counter has reached the threshold; its source is the Choice that reached it).
3. **The next card**, drawn as above with the game as it stands after the Event and the round step.
4. **Milestones** up to the next card's date, in date order with the Event first on a tie, each resolved at its moment (Condition, Slot); after a tipping point they pass silently, except those listed in the beat's `**Milestones:**` field (the runaway beat lists `sota-asi`, so a runaway before 2038 still reaches superintelligence). Every milestone that moves the gauge is shown (v5.4; before, only the last), the game's first **slowdown** is always shown (v5.2: beside a gauge mover if they share a stretch, so a card about AI stalling never follows a hidden slowdown), and beside the movers the latest other milestone; later slowdowns and the rest are quiet (applied, never shown or logged). With no mover, one milestone is shown. The same resolution (`resolveTo`) is what `capabilityAt` uses before the card is drawn.

The UI shows the steps in order and calls `applyStep()` for each, so `state` always matches the screen.

### The capability gauge

A graph of AI capability over `Gauge years` (2000–2064), on a log scale, filled in up to the year the game has reached. Its pace follows `Gauge pace` (slow before 2010, about 4 to 5 times a year since, from Epoch AI's measurements), continues at that trend after 2026, and bends with the game (`gaugeBends(state)`): each milestone reached (shown or quiet) multiplies the pace from its date by its `Pace`, rising by its `Growth` each year after (recursive self-improvement 3% and superintelligence 5% a year, until a slowdown stops it), and (v5.4) each major Choice, one whose Brake is 1 or more either way, by 1 − `Gauge brake pace` × its Brake (0.5 to 1.5), × 0.85 more if it sets `restraint` or `pause`. Minor Choices don't bend it. `Engine.gaugeHeights(state, steps)` (256 steps, a sample every quarter year, so every whole year is a sample) scales the climb so the trend alone reaches `Gauge trend height` by the last year, with no ceiling (v5.6): a runaway curve rises above 1. (v5.7) The UI's `gaugeHeights()` then scales the whole curve down evenly until its highest point fits (no knee, so no plateau a decision didn't cause; the chevron is gone). The engine's heights, which `check-story.js` tests, are unscaled, so the filled past never changes in the data, though a rescale redraws it smaller. `paceOf(state)` (v5.7) is the world's pace (racing, paced, held, leveled, beyond); `pace` condition terms test it at the item's date, and cards with pace terms count as capability cards (`CAPPED`). Milestones with `Shown: always` are never made quiet (`transitionSteps`). `Engine.outlook(state, untilYear)` (v5.6) resolves the milestones still to come with no further Choices (`resolveTo` on a copy) and returns the next one or two that move the gauge or slow AI, each with its Ahead line, and a `status` (`beyond`, `held`, `level` or `steady`) for when none is left. **The filled part, the past, never changes:** bends start on whole years at or after the fill, and `check-story.js` redraws the curve after every step of 3,000 games and fails if any height at or before the fill year moved. Milestones whose Counts as names another (`sota-robots-late` counts as `sota-robots`) satisfy `reached` terms for it (`isReached`). Each shown milestone's log entry carries `why`, its Because line (`becauseFor`). `state.gauge` (the level name under the graph) is an index into `Gauge levels` (Tools, Expert, AGI, Self-improving, Superintelligence), moved by milestones whose Gauge names a level.

**When it moves (game.js):** the fill grows only as years pass, with the Interlude's count (to the next card's year), never at the card draw's own moment or at an Event panel (an Event shows what AI can do now). A major Choice calls `updateGauge` with the year already reached, over 3 s while "As a result…" shows, so only the projection flattens or steepens. The Future fills to 2060 (UI TEXT → Future dateline), leaving a sliver of projection to 2064.

**Milestones shown (v5.4, v5.8; v6.1: in the Interlude's count, each with its box):** between two cards, every milestone that moves the gauge is shown (recursive self-improvement and superintelligence can share a stretch), with the game's first slowdown beside them and any `Shown: always` milestone (robots catching up). Since v5.8 the others pass quietly beside a mover; with no mover, one is shown.

**Versions (v5.4):** `versioned(state, item, cap)` returns a card or Event as it reads in this world: the first `### Version` whose When holds (at the item's date) replaces its Headline, Timeline label, Place, Summary, Story or Question. Cards are versioned when drawn (`selectScenario`, `transitionOdds`), Events when their step is resolved (`resolveTo`).

**Certain Events (v6.1):** `eventOdds` shows an Event marked Certain (p 1) whenever its Appears when holds after a Choice of its Round, before Likelihood and past `Most Events per game` and the one-transition gap, as it does for near misses; `eventPool` leaves them out. The UI opens its full Story (`showEventPanel`) and waits for a reading (3 s + 30 ms a character of the summary, at most 12 s) before Continue.

**Near misses (v5.4):** a near miss sets `near_miss` and `near_<type>` (war, collapse or runaway) and `state.nearMissNew`; `eventOdds` then forces the matching Near miss Event, dated a year after the card.

## Screens and flow (`site/js/game.js`)

Text that fades in on cue is first hidden with `resetFade()`, which is **instant**, so nothing is ever seen fading away before its reveal; it's then revealed with `.in` (`stagger()`). All timed sequences go through `wait()` / `waitForTap()`, which check a `flow` counter, so Home and Play again cancel cleanly. Every Continue, Choice and Play again fires only once. Screens crossfade over ~1 s (`--screen`). Text reveals take ~700 ms (`--slow`), ~250 ms apart (`GAP`), with `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`. Nothing appears or disappears instantly.

0. **Home** (`#home`, `showHome()`; v5.7: leaving a game before its Future is revealed pauses it, `pause()` holds every `wait()` timer until `resume()`, its overlays and gauge are kept in `held`, and Home shows **Continue** (`continueGame()`) and **Restart** (`restartGame()`, no Disclaimer) instead of Play; once the Future is revealed (`gameLive` false) Home is as before): the history Timeline (`homeTimeline()`: every entry evenly spaced on one horizontal line, dates and titles alternating above and below, each dot colored along the Round colors, ending at Now · 2026; hover or tap an entry for its line; it drifts once from the oldest entry to Now on the first visit), the title, the tagline, **Play**, **Explore** (v5.8, the Futures collection) and **About**, and the capability gauge in the corner (at Now, without any game's milestones). **About** (`#about`) is a reading page with a sticky Back (and Escape) and a Play button. The results' **Home** button comes back here.
1. **Disclaimer** (`play()`): fades in (~2.1 s), holds 5 s, fades out (2.2 s), then `startGame()`.
2. **Game start** (`startGame()`, also Play again): the strip and gauge fade in, the deck (6 Round cards and the Future) slides in, the first card is dealt.
3. **Rounds** (the "table" screen). Desktop: deck | card | reading column. Phones: small deck top-left, card centered, text below.
   - **Colors.** `cardColor(item)`: a Scenario its round's color, an Event the Event color, a milestone the State of the art color, a tipping point the tipping color. `setAccent()` sets `--accent` and `--accent-text` (lightened to 4.5:1 if needed).
   - **Draw:** the top card slides off the deck, pauses, then 3D-flips (~900 ms) with the flip sound peaking edge-on.
   - **Scenario:** "Round N of 6" above the card; beside it the dateline, the headline, the summary, **Read the full story** (`setupToggle()`), the question and 3 shuffled Choices.
   - **Choice → Consequence** (`settleChoice()`), with no jump: the other two Choices fade in place, then the chosen one glides up (FLIP). **"As a result…"** sentences fade in one at a time, then **Continue**, and the card flies onto the strip (`FLY_MS` 1.4 s), crossfading into its marker.
   - **Tipping beat** (`showTipping()`), when `Engine.choose` returns a beat: instead of "As a result…", a full-screen overlay (`#tipping`, above everything, 97.5% dark with a faint wash of the tipping color) fades in over 2.4 s with "The tipping point · {year}", the headline (`#tp-headline`) and three titled parts (v5.6; v5.8: underlined serif titles clearer than the softer body, and it never scrolls by itself): what happened (the beat's Text or Version), why your decision tipped the scale (the Choice's Tipping why) and whether it could have been stopped (the Prevented line). v5.8: a paragraph at a time, like the Future's story: each part's title just before its first paragraph, then after each paragraph "tap to continue" (`#tp-hint`) and a tap on the overlay, or after a slow reading (3 s + 45 ms a character) it goes on by itself; then **Continue** (phase `tipping`). The music dips while it shows, and the wash breathes (`tp-mourn`). Then the card flies to the strip and the tipping point's shatter (`shatterSvg()`) lands just after it (`TIP_OFFSET`, 0.4 years, so it isn't hidden behind the card), labeled with the beat's Timeline label, as fragments of the line burst outward and fade (`shatterLine()`, cosmetic `Math.random`).
   - **The transition** (`transition()`), in the engine's order: **Event panel** (`showEventPanel()`: the "Out of your hands" panel with Read more and Continue; v5.7: with Read more open it glides to the window's center, `placeEventPanel()`, `.ep-open`, 48 px clear above and below, and back on Show less; its marker fades onto the strip afterward), the round step (silent), then the **Interlude** (v6.1, `interlude(opts)`): a large year (`#il-year`, an odometer, `setYear()`) counts from the last date shown to the next card's (`count()`), quickly at first and slowing as the last digit falls into place (the inverse of an ease-out over 1.2 s + 0.5 s × √years), with `Sound.tick()` for each year. As the count reaches a milestone's year it rests (`COUNT_HOLD`, 1.5 s): the milestone's box (`sotaBox()`: label, Short definition and its Because line) joins the column on the right (`#il-sotas`, a second one below it; on phones, under the year), its diamond lands and the gauge glows or pulses (`landMilestone(item, level, true)`, no caption by the gauge). The graph fills with the count, a segment at a time (`updateGauge(…, "out")`). Then the opener, then **Continue** (`#il-continue`): nothing moves on by itself, and a tap before Continue finishes the count at once. Dates that aren't exact years show "Years pass." instead of counting. Milestones dated before an Event get their own count up to the Event (no opener), then its panel; with none, the panel just opens. Before the Future it counts to 2060 under UI TEXT → Before the Future ("{n} years pass.", n = 2060 less the last date shown). During the Interlude the strip and gauge sit above its dimming (`body.il-lift`).
4. **Timeline strip:** (v5.9) its background is solid down to just below the lowest label under the line (`--solid-to`, set at the end of `layoutMarks()`), then an eased fade over the rest of its height, which glides (1.4 s) as labels arrive, so an emptier strip dissolves over a longer distance. (v6.0) The background is its own layer, `#strip-bg`, just after `#strip` (z-index 5, under the strip's 6; 8 and 9 during the Interlude): it follows `.strip.on` and `.strip.tied` by sibling selectors and gets `--solid-to` and `--away` from game.js, so at the Future reveal it slides up opaque while only the labels and marks fade, and the story scrolling underneath never shows through them. A gentle scroll (`gentleScroll()`) stops as soon as the player scrolls (wheel, touch or a scrolling key).
4. **Timeline strip:** one linear time scale (`stripX()`) from 2017 to 2050 (or 3 years past the latest dated item); on phones it starts just before Now (`PHONE_LEAD`, 1.2 years) and hides the history dots and labels, so the player's 2027 to 2050 gets the whole width, and the phone strip is 238 px tall so a third row of labels below the line fits; (v5.6) labels wrap onto two balanced lines at 118 px on desktop (`stripLabel()` keeps hyphenated words whole), so the desktop strip is 250 px tall with the line at 116 px; history dots, Now, and the player's cards, Events, milestones and tipping points. Markers: Scenarios tiny card faces (`--mark`; hover or tap for the Choice made on it), Events grayscale (`--mark-event`, dashed leader), milestones diamonds (`--mark-sota`; hover or tap for the Short definition and why it happened), tipping points a dark disc (`--mark-tip`; hover for the beat's headline). `bindTip(target, getText)` takes a string or `{ head, text, why, color }`; `logFor(item)` finds the item's log entry. Every marker always shows its year (in its own color) and label (`layoutMarks()`: rows above and below the line in date order, leaders that never cross, nothing overlapping or off screen, history labels dropped before any player label). At the end the strip is tied to the scroll (`updateStripAway()`) and rises away as the Future card rises toward the results. (v6.0) It stays still until See what you chose opens, since the page can scroll only a few pixels past the story and a strip that moved with them looked like a glitch; from then on the rise starts from the scroll position at that moment (`awayBase`), so it never jumps.
5. **The capability gauge** (`#gauge`, top right): the graph described under Engine, its level name, a caption for each milestone, and a dropdown on hover or tap with the latest milestone's definition and why it happened (or where AI is now), then **Ahead** (v5.6, `fillAhead()`): the next expected milestone, its year and its Ahead line, recomputed each time it opens (on Home, for a new game). A curve that would leave the top of the chart rescales the chart instead (v5.7). While the pace is held, the level name reads UI TEXT → Gauge held label.
6. **Future reveal.** The music dips. `chooseFuture(state)` picks the Future and a story: `Engine.chooseStory(state, future, last)` (v6.0) tells, of the stories that fit the world (`Engine.storiesFor`, v5.3: a story's `When`), the one with **Drawn by** the game points to most (`storyPull`: each entry the game has set × Continuity decay ^ rounds since), if it reaches Story pull minimum, unless it was told last time and another is within three quarters of its pull; otherwise one of the stories without Drawn by at random, avoiding the one shown last time (`localStorage` key `amot-story-<futureId>`). `storyText` fills a `{world}` paragraph with the story's World line. The last card is drawn slowly with a strong glow and shows the story's image; the strip recolors; then 2060, the title, the tagline, **How we got here** (up to three sentences, slowly), and the story one paragraph at a time. Then **See what you chose** and **Play again**; the phase `reveal-done` waits (`settled()`) until every paragraph and the buttons have fully faded in (v5.9: under load, the last paragraph could still be fading).
7. **See what you chose** (v5.6: Values first): **Six hidden values** (bars of the scaled Values, ±6 filling the half-width; expandable explanations; after a tipping point the lost four are grayed out and struck through at `state.snapshot`, with their After the tipping point line), then the player's Timeline from Now (the 3 latest history entries fading upward) through their cards, Events, tipping point (a dark entry tagged "The tipping point") and milestones, ending at the Future card; balanced left and right on desktop; recolors to the Future. Under each Choice's "As a result…", `valuePills()` shows a pill per Value it moved (the Value's color and name, then the applied change in white), struck through where it no longer counted. (v6.0) An Event shows pills too, from its log entry's `applied` (its Effects, plus Near miss effects for a near miss's Event), and each Value's explanation says what the player's Choices did (`choiceSum()`, the pills' total) and, when half a point or more came from elsewhere (Events, near misses, milestones, era drift), a second line, UI TEXT → Moved besides your choices, so the sentences agree with the pills wherever the bar lands. The tipping entry shows its year, headline and Tipping why, with Read more for what happened and whether it could have been stopped (the Timeline is relaid every frame while it opens, `relayoutFor()`); its dot is the shatter. An Event has no card there, only a small grey rectangle (`.atl-evmark`, v5.8). v5.8: no separate State of the art section, and See what you chose scrolls down until the whole Values table is in view (`gentleScroll(…, "values")`).
8a. **Futures collection** (v5.8, `#futures`, `showFutures()`): Explore on Home. Every Future in file order as an empty frame (`.fcell-frame`) until reached, then its story's card, title (in its accent) and tagline; a tally (UI TEXT → Collection tally). `revealFuture()` records the Future, its story and that game's Ending line (`rememberFuture()`, `localStorage` `amot-found`); clicking a found one opens `#fs-panel` with the story as it was read, Back or Escape to close.
8. **Play again** favors unseen cards in the same session and skips the Disclaimer.

## Test hooks (in `site/js/game.js`; never active without the URL parameter)

- **`?seed=N`**: every random choice in the game (card draws, Events, Choice order, story picks) comes from one seeded generator (`Engine.random`, mulberry32 in engine.js). The same seed, the same choices and the same window size replay a game exactly. Nothing in the game logic calls `Math.random()` directly (only the cosmetic particles and sound variation do).
- **`?test=1`**: test mode. Home and the Disclaimer are skipped and audio never starts. Everything runs about **10 times faster**, not instantly: `wait()` timers and scrolls divide by `SPEED`, Web Animations are shortened, and CSS transitions and animations get `playbackRate = 10`. **`&speed=N`** sets another rate (`&speed=1` is real time).
- Test mode exposes a read-only **`window.__game`**: `seed`, `phase` and `phaseId` (what the player can do now: `busy`, `choose`, `continue`, `event`, `tipping`, `interlude`, `reveal-done`; each change gets a new id), `game`, `round`, `values` (scaled, as the bars show them), `rawValues`, `flags`, `agents`, `tipping` (`{ id, type, text, why, prevented }` once tipped), `snapshot` and `valuesLost` (v5.6), `timeline` (every Scenario, Event, tipping point and milestone shown so far: `{ type: scenario | event | tipping | milestone, id, date, year, label }`), `gauge`, `future` (`{ id, title, story, lookingBack, paragraphs }`), `picks` (each card drawn: `round`, `id`, `fresh`, `afterHumanity`, `tipped`, `lead`), `sessionSeen`, `futures`.
- A game only enters a phase once what it shows has fully appeared (`settled()`), so a phase means "ready".


## Look, sound and motion

- Background `--bg` `#161614`. Cormorant Garamond for display text, Future stories, How we got here, Consequences and the tipping beat; Inter for body text, summaries and openers.
- **Particles** (`site/js/particles.js`): a star field (~620 stars on desktop, ~280 on phones) in three layers, pulsing gently and pushed away from the pointer. Paused when hidden; static with reduced motion.
- **Sound** (`site/js/audio.js`), all generated: ambient chords and sparse bells (a dip during the Future and the tipping beat), a soft click on buttons, a two-note confirm on Choices, a swoosh on flips. Audio starts at the first gesture unless the browser allows it earlier. Mute is remembered (`amot-muted`).
- **Reduced motion:** fades instead of slides and flips, no float, pulse or hover lifts.
- Check layout at 1440×900 (first) and 390×844 after changes; the harness does both.
