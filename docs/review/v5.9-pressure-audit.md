**Bug found.** `sota-unheld` fires when `not flag pause`. Restraint never sets `pause`, so every restraint-held world shows "The pause is lifted" in 2047 with nothing the player did. In those worlds `r6-cap` (the only card that can lift the hold) can't appear, because it needs the moratorium. v5.9 fixes both. Lifting needs a new flag, `lifted`, set only by a lift Choice, and `r6-cap` appears in any held world.

**Cards most often drawn in held worlds** (per game, among 3,914 held games): r5-launch 2,240, ev-cables 2,220, ev-weights 1,740, r6-arms 1,131, r6-cap 1,055, r6-slowtreaty 941, r4-factories 838, r4-shutdown 727, r4-rogue 574, **r5-bust 572**, **r5-checkers 195**.
- **r5-bust and r5-checkers** in a held world say AI "stalled", "never did" surpass people, which is false there: it was stopped, not stalled. They need `pace not held` or a held Version.
- **r6-slowtreaty** ("AI stuck near human level") has the same problem in restraint-held worlds. It needs `pace not held`.
- **r4-rogue, r4-shutdown and ev-weights** are plausible at the held level (AGI-class systems exist), but none mentions the cap. I'd give each a short held Version: the flaw-finder was built under the cap, the London AI is a capped system, and the stolen blueprint is the strongest system the cap allows (which is why it's worth stealing).

## New mechanics

- **Slow world** = `pace is held` or `pace is leveled` at the card's date. Conditions: `pace not racing, pace not paced, pace not beyond`.
- **Pressure cards** (SETTINGS): r4-letitrun, r5-defense, r5-moonshot, r6-climateai, r6-cap, r5-antitrust, r6-election. From round 5 (2046), a slow-world game that hasn't met one narrows to them, after any card answering an Event and after the pace question.
- **New flags**
  - `lifted`: the line is lifted outright.
  - `relaunch`: public money restarts the climb after the bust.
  - `exempt_medicine`, `exempt_defense`, `exempt_climate`: one field opened, the rest still capped. Each also sets `paced`.
  - `people_said_no`: the public broke up or dissolved the AI companies.
- **New milestones** (fixed dates, Slot `lift`, `Shown: always`)
  - **The pause is lifted**: 2042, 2047 (existing) and 2055. Condition `reached sota-held, flag lifted`.
  - **One field opened** (new): 2042, 2047 and 2055. Condition `reached sota-held` or `reached sota-plateau`, plus any `exempt_*`. Its Because names the field ("You opened the cap for medicine in {year}"). Pace about 4, so the curve rises gently.
  - **AI climbs again** (new): 2048 and 2055. Condition `reached sota-plateau, flag relaunch`. Sets pace `paced`.
  - **Open question:** I still have to check that milestones dated after the last card (2055) are shown in the sweep to the Future. If they aren't, a 2053 lift only bends the projection, and the gauge's Ahead says AI is expected to climb again.
- **`exempt_defense`** adds to the war tipping point's conditions or the odds of ev-nearwar, so the defense carve-out is never the safe middle option.
