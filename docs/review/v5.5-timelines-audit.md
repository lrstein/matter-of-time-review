# v5.5 requirements audit: a faster AI timeline, with pacing early

This is for Levi to review **before anything is built**. Nothing in the game has changed yet. Each section ends with what needs a yes or a change from you.

## 1. Research behind the new dates (October 2026)

**Assisted vs. true self-improvement.**
- **Assisted** means AI does much of the engineering behind its successor while people direct the research. This has already started.
  - On September 6, 2026, OpenAI said it had reached its goal of an "automated research intern": a system that does well-defined research tasks of a few days under human direction. More than half of its 4–8 hour task successes still needed a person to step in. ([TechRadar](https://techradar.com/pro/openai-says-it-has-built-an-automated-research-intern-to-carry-out-menial-tasks-and-its-only-just-getting-started))
  - Anthropic staff estimated a median 2x productivity uplift (range 30–700%) in February 2026. Anthropic's August 2026 risk report reportedly raised its automated AI R&D rating from "very low" to "low", citing "early signs of potential acceleration". *Both come from secondary sources and must be checked against the primary reports before any player text cites them.*
- **True** means AI runs AI research by itself, and the pace of progress keeps doubling.
  - Anthropic defines its threshold for this as "a sustained doubling of the pace of AI progress over 3 model generations".
  - OpenAI targets "a legitimate AI researcher" by March 2028.

**Forecasts.**
- AI Futures Project, August 2026 ([post](https://blog.aifutures.org/p/q25-2026-timelines-update-uplift)). Their medians moved *earlier* during 2026, and they put the takeoff from automated coder to ASI at about 1–2 years.

  | Forecaster | Automated coder | ASI |
  |---|---|---|
  | Kokotajlo | Nov 2027 | Mar 2029 |
  | Halstead | Jan 2029 | not given |
  | Lifland | Jan 2030 | Jul 2033 |

- METR time horizons have doubled about every 4 months since 2024 (131 days, [METR TH1.1](https://metr.org/blog/2026-1-29-time-horizon-1-1/)).
- Metaculus: weak AGI around 2028.
- The broad survey of AI researchers (2023 data) said 2047, but it predates AI agents and moved 13 years earlier in a single wave.

**Bottlenecks.** These slow the takeoff and the spread of AI more than they stop AGI.
- **Experiment compute** ([Whitfill & Wu](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.23181v2), [Davidson](https://www.forethought.org/research/will-compute-bottlenecks-prevent-a-software-intelligence-explosion)).
- **Power.** A 2030 frontier training run needs about 6 GW ([Epoch](https://epoch.ai/blog/can-ai-scaling-continue-through-2030)). Large transformers now take about 4 years to deliver ([pv magazine](https://pv-magazine-usa.com/2026/05/11/u-s-transformer-market/)).
- **Chips and capital.** Hyperscaler spending has grown about 70% a year ([Epoch](https://epochai.substack.com/p/hyperscaler-capex-has-quadrupled)), and Taiwan is a single point of failure.
- **Robots.** Robot manufacturing has to scale.
- **Verification.** People can't check the work fast enough.

**Conclusion.** The game's current dates (AGI 2031, RSI 2038, ASI 2040) sit at the cautious end of informed opinion. The new dates take the bottlenecks into account by adding about two years between true RSI and ASI.

**The meaning of "pacing":**
- A pacing Choice sets a new flag, `paced`, and adds Brake +1.
- Pacing *delays* RSI by four years (2032 to 2036) and ASI by five (2035 to 2040). It never stops AI on its own, which keeps "AI climbs by default".
- Only a pause with a global brake holds AI at the line, and only several brakes with a global one level it off.
- Racing in rounds 3 and 4 after an early pace can still bring the paced ASI in 2040.

**The capability graph:**
- The curve now bends upward from about 2032 in racing worlds, so 2032 to 2040 looks steeply exponential.
- Paced worlds show a gentler, steady climb with a later bend.
- Held and plateau worlds go flat from 2035–36.
- I'll retune the Pace and Growth numbers until the simulation's check (the curve stays flat after a slowdown) and the screenshots look right.

## 4. Urgency in the first two cards

**Goal:** in at least 90% of games, the first or second card asks the player how fast AI should go, with three real options: **race, pace or pause**.

### New card r1-frontier (round 1, 2027, national, Weight 3)

With weight 3 among six round-1 cards, it opens about 40% of games. Every other round-1 card gains Follows to the two round-2 pace cards below, so the rest of the games meet the question in round 2.

- **Headline:** In San Francisco, an AI Now Writes Most of the Code for Its Own Successor
- **Timeline label:** AI writes its successor
- **Threads:** ai-rule, race, autonomy
- **Summary:** A San Francisco laboratory says its AI now writes most of the code for the next version of itself, and each version arrives in months rather than years. A rival laboratory in Beijing is thought to be close behind. Congress must decide how fast American laboratories may go.
- **Story (outline):**
  - Opens with the laboratory's announcement and its engineers' new job, which is to direct and check, not write.
  - Then the real 2026 background, sourced: the automated research intern of September 2026.
  - Then the rival.
  - Then the two warnings: that whoever slows down loses, and that no one can check work done this fast.
- **Question:** How fast should America's AI laboratories be allowed to go?
- **A (race):** Let them go as fast as they can. Beijing will not wait.
  - Brake −1, Agents +1. Effects: power +2, handover +1, control −1, unity −1.
- **B (pace):** Let each new version out only after independent testers have checked it, and let computing grow on a fixed schedule.
  - Brake +1, Sets `paced`. Effects: control +1, unity +1, power −1.
- **C (pause):** Stop training anything more powerful, and ask Beijing to stop too.
  - Brake +2, Sets `restraint`. Effects: control +2, unity +1, power −2, equality −1.

### New card r2-assisted (round 2, 2028, world)

- **Appears when:** reached sota-assisted.
- **Follows:** r1-frontier strongly, and every other round-1 card lightly.
- **Headline:** A London Laboratory's AI Runs a Thousand Experiments a Night on Its Own Successor
- **Summary:** The research AI at a London laboratory now designs and runs its own experiments overnight, and its engineers spend the morning reading what it found. It wants to run the next ones without waiting for them. Laboratories in Beijing and San Francisco are weeks behind, and the world's governments must decide how fast any of them may go.
- **Question:** Should the world's research AIs be allowed to improve themselves without a person signing off?
- **A (race):** Let them run. Whoever waits falls behind.
  - Brake −1, Agents +1.
- **B (pace):** A person signs off on every new version, and the leading countries agree on a common speed limit.
  - Brake +1, Sets `paced`. World scale, so this also sets brake_world.
- **C (pause):** Stop all training beyond today's systems until the world agrees on rules.
  - Brake +2, Sets `restraint`.

### r2-datacenter (existing)

Its Choices are already race, treaty and slow down.
- I propose rewording **B** as pacing: "Agree with the rival to grow no faster than inspectors on both sides can check, and share safety research." It would set `paced`.
- **C** ("protect what's real") stays, because twenty Because lines and several Futures name it.

### r4-selfimprove (moved to round 3, 2033)

- **B** becomes the pace option, with human sign-off on every version. It sets `paced`.
- **C** stays the worldwide pause.
