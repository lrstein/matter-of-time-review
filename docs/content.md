# site/content/story.md — the content

Read this before editing `site/content/story.md` (it was `content/CLAUDE.md` until the launch layout). The project overview and terminology are in the root `CLAUDE.md`; to add a card, use the `add-card` skill.

## Writing rules (standing rules for all player-facing text)

They also sit at the top of `story.md`. They apply to everything a player reads (headlines, summaries, stories, questions, Choices, results, openers, tipping beats, milestone text, Future titles, taglines, Grounded by sentences and stories, Values, UI text, About and history), but not to image descriptions or the notes in story.md.

- **Punctuation:** no colons, semicolons, em dashes or en dashes, and no spaced hyphens. Hyphens inside words ("drug-resistant") are fine, and so is a colon between digits ("2:14 a.m."). **American spelling** (center, color, program, neighbor, organize, defense, gray, toward). The linter strips links before checking and must report **0 problems**.
- **Register:** a careful New York Times news story, for an intelligent general reader, never juvenile. Clear, precise and elegant; an advanced word only when it's the best word. Open with who, where and what happened, and explain any unfamiliar idea in plain words the first time. No far-fetched or invented-sounding lines. **Headlines** in title case, naming the places and people involved. **Timeline labels** are two to five words.
- **Ground every story in the competition** between companies and countries. In a world of superhuman AI, any person, company or country without it is at a disadvantage.
- **Openers** (lead-ins) must follow logically from what came before, with no forced reasoning. Each Grounded by sentence must make the link from that decision to that Future obvious.
- **Sources:** every real past fact or statistic links to a source, written as `[visible words](https://…)`: a major outlet, a primary source or Wikipedia. **Never invent a source**, and check that the page says what the sentence claims. (In v5, major outlets that block automated fetching were replaced by Wikipedia pages that could be checked.)
- **Lengths:** a Summary is 2 or 3 sentences, enough to decide on. "As a result" stays short (about three sentences). Future stories are about 400 to 470 words and end in a scene, not a summary.
- **Names:** Futures use "The" only where it sounds right (The Garden, The Gilded Ones, The Oracle, The Long Middle, The Last War, The Pause; Common Ground, Afterlife, Elsewhere, Blackout, Glass Houses, Two Kinds, Inheritance, A Second Chance, Overgrowth go without).
- **Trust the reader**: don't moralize. **Leave real tension**: no obvious good Choice, no purely good or bad Future. Concrete before abstract, varied rhythm.
- **Agents cards** (any card whose Appears when requires `flag agents_era`): the decision is about AI systems (agents dividing a shipping lane, a grid AI choosing which data centers stay on, an AI asking to copy itself); people appear only in passing, as a detail or a number; the Choices are framed from the systems' side (efficiency, reliability, agreements between systems); the question still uses the plain second person. **Never write a line like "humans no longer matter."** It should only be felt.
- **After humanity cards** use the same quiet framing: what the remaining systems do with the animals, the oceans, the empty cities, the seed vault, the archive.
- **Image** (launch, October 2026): every card's `**Image:**` is empty for now, so every front is the placeholder face. The 14 cards that had art keep its path in `**Archived image:** images/cards/<id>.webp` under the empty Image line (the parser ignores the field), and the files are in `image-studio/published/`. To restore one, move its file back to `site/images/cards/` and copy the path back into Image. Image paths are relative to `site/`.
- **Image descriptions** (every card, Event, milestone and Future story, even while images are on hold): follow the notes in IMAGE STYLE GUIDE. "Foreground… Middle ground… Background… No text or lettering." (v5.6) A snapshot of ordinary life at the card's date, not a staged scene: clothes, phones, cars and buildings of that year (2027–2031 looks like today), no humanoid or household robots unless the card is about them or guarantees them, and the scene itself carries the metaphor, with open symbolism only for subjects that can't be shown physically (self-improvement, superintelligence, a software flaw).
- **"As a result" never assumes the future** (v5.6). It tells only what follows within about a year of the card's date, at the card's own scale (a national card's result is about your country). It never names a year after the card's date, never says "within five years", "for decades", "eventually", "every year" or "for the rest of their lives", and never settles what a later card, milestone or the Future decides (who wins a race, whether AI reaches a level, a company's or a generation's fate). `check-story.js` flags later years and these phrases.
- **News register** (v5.6). A Scenario's or Event's Story reads like a New York Times news story: a lede that says who did what, where and when ("…said on a Tuesday", never "on Tuesday", since the date is only a year), then the specifics (numbers, institutions, how the thing works, with any term explained once), then the sourced history that puts it in context, then reactions attributed by role, then what has to be decided. No narrative scene-setting ("Every evening at six…") and no personifying the AI ("it has begun to ask, in effect, why…"); report what it did. After humanity cards keep their quiet framing but state deaths plainly ("died", never "were gone").
- **Invented names in italics** (v5.7). Every invented company, system, product or publication is written in italics in player text, `*Larkspur*` (rendered by `rich()` and `inline()`; asterisks are stripped wherever italics can't show), and is introduced with "a", never "the": "*Larkspur*, a San Francisco research laboratory", not "*Larkspur*, the San Francisco laboratory". "The" tells the reader they should already know it, as they would a real institution; "a" and the italics mark it as part of an imagined future. Real organizations stay in roman type. Reports by real organizations are fine, since every card is dated in the future and its lede says "on a Monday", not "on Monday".
- **Plain reading** (v5.7). Compression is not elegance: no pronoun or "one" whose referent is far back or ambiguous ("still others to theirs"), no dropped verbs ("the other side's had"), no riddles ("Every search for all of it found less than all of it"). Say the thing.
- **Invented names** (v5.6). Name invented companies, systems and places the way their founders would, so they read like a real firm and not a play on one: a surname, a place, a plain word, a word from the local language, occasionally a Latin or Greek root. Avoid "Nexus", "Quantum", "Synth", "-ify", "Open-" and anything echoing a real lab. Record every name in **Names** below, and reuse it when a later card mentions the same thing.
- **Dates** fall within their round's years, and every Event sits between the rounds around it (see Chronology). **No more than 9 years between two cards in a row, or between the last card and the Future (2060)** (v5.5; it was 7 in v5.3): the rounds are 2027, 2028–29, 2030–34, 2039–41, 2046 and 2053. Round 1 is about what the AI of 2027 is already doing; the race, pace or pause question belongs to rounds 2 and 3 (Pace cards). When a card's date moves, check every duration in it and in the cards and stories that mention it ("for six years", "a decade later").

## `site/content/story.md` format

The parser follows these rules exactly.

- `# SECTION` starts a section: `SCALE`, `SETTINGS`, `UI TEXT`, `ABOUT`, `HISTORY TIMELINE`, `VALUES`, `IMAGE STYLE GUIDE`, `ROUND 1`…`ROUND 6`, `EVENTS`, `STATE OF THE ART`, `TIPPING POINTS`, `FUTURES`. Unknown sections and loose text are ignored.
- `## id` starts an item. `### Choice A/B/C` starts a Choice; `### Story N` a Future story; `### Version name` (v5.4) a version of a card or Event.
- `**Label:** value` sets a field, continuing onto following lines until the next label, heading or `---`. Multi-paragraph fields split on blank lines. **A line wholly in brackets is an editor's note and is skipped**, so a multi-paragraph note must have each paragraph on its own bracketed line.
- **Links:** `[visible words](https://url)`, kept as written and rendered by the UI (`rich()`) as links that open in a new tab; where a link can't work (headlines, Choices, openers, the strip, tooltips) `plain()` keeps the words. A URL containing parentheses must encode them (`%28`, `%29`).
- **Dates:** `yearOf(date)`: an exact year stays; "Late 2030s" is 2038, "Mid" +5, "Early" +2, a bare decade ("2040s") is 2045.
- **Effects** and **Targets**: `power +1, control -2` (decimals allowed; `(none)` means none). Values: power, control, handover, unity, reality, equality.
- **Conditions** (Appears when, Openers, Requires, Condition, Likelihood, Tipping point): comma-separated terms, all of which must hold: `pace is held` / `pace not racing` (v5.7: the world's pace at the item's date, racing, paced, held, leveled or beyond, from the latest milestone with a Sets pace, else `paced` if that flag is set, else racing), `power >= 2`, `unity <= 0` (also `year`, `round`, `agents`, `brakes`), `flag X`, `not flag X`, `last flag X` (set by the most recent Choice or the Event after it; no card uses it since v5.1), `any flag a, b, c` (the list runs on until a term that isn't a bare flag name), `capability >= AGI` / `capability <= Expert` (a Gauge level, at the card's own date) and `reached sota-robots` / `not reached sota-asi` (a milestone reached, shown or quiet, by the card's date; in a milestone's Condition, by its own date). Parsed as `[{ type: value|flag|any|cap|reached, … }]`.
- **Scenario fields:** Headline, Timeline label, Date (an exact year), Place, **Round** (`3`, `5-6`), **Threads**, **Scale**, **Follows**, optional **Appears when**, **Openers** (lines `- condition | sentence`, ending `- default | sentence`), optional **Weight** (how likely it is to be drawn, default 1), **After humanity** (`yes`), optional **Added** (`v5.4`, the version that added it; used only by the review documents), Image, Image description, Summary, Story, Question, and three Choices with Text, Effects, optional **Sets**, **Agents**, **Brake** (v5.4, signed: how much it slows AI's money, power or pace, `+0.5`, `+1`, `+2` for a pause, or speeds it up, `-0.5` to `-2` for racing; 1 or more either way makes it a major Choice that bends the graph's projection), optional **Clears** (named flags it ends, like `pause` when the cap is allowed to expire), optional **Demands response** (v6.0, `yes`: the very next card must answer it, one whose openers or conditions name it, with no Event between; used by the halt, r2-assisted/C, answered by r3-geneva), **Tipping point** (`conditions | war|collapse|runaway`), As a result.
- **Event fields:** Headline, Timeline label, Date, Place, **Threads**, **Round** (the rounds after whose Choice it can happen), optional Appears when, **Likelihood** (`base 0.15; +0.10 if equality <= -2; +0.15 if flag r3-drones/A`; semicolons are fine here, it isn't player text), optional Sets, optional **Brake** (as on a Choice; its Scale decides whether it is global), optional **Demands response** (`yes`: the next card must answer it, one whose openers or conditions name it), optional **Near miss** (`war`, `collapse` or `runaway`: shown, always, right after a near miss of that kind, dated a year after the card), optional **Certain** (v6.1, `yes`: shown whenever its Appears when holds after a Choice of its Round, past Likelihood and the limits on Events; its panel opens with the full Story, and Continue waits for a reading; used by ev-vienna, the world signing the pause r4-selfimprove/C called for), Image, Image description, Effects, Summary, Story, and optional versions.
- **Milestone fields:** Headline, Timeline label, Date, **Gauge** (a level or `hold`), **Pace** (how it bends the capability curve), Short definition, **Because** (v5.2: lines `- flag r2-datacenter/A | Reached on time. In {year} you chose to race…`, ending `- default | …`, chosen like openers and shown on hover as why it happened), optional Slot and Condition, optional **Counts as** (another milestone's id, so `reached` terms for it hold: `sota-robots-late` counts as `sota-robots`), optional **Slowdown** (`yes`), optional **Sets pace** (v5.7: the world's pace from it on: racing, paced, held, leveled, beyond), optional **Shown** (`always`, v5.7: never made quiet, because later cards assume it), optional **Growth** (v5.4: how much its Pace rises each year after it, until a slowdown; recursive self-improvement uses 0.03, superintelligence 0.05), Effects, optional Weight (multiplies Effects), What happened, What it means, How life changes.
- **`{year}`** (v5.2) in an opener, a Because line or a Grounded by sentence becomes the year the player made the decision its condition names (for Grounded by, the date of the matching card or Event). Use it to tie a sentence to the player's own history: "the cap you signed in {year}". A line with `{year}` needs a flag in its condition; `check-story.js` fails on any left unfilled. Write Because lines that credit a decision that really moved AI (racing, releasing, rescuing) and say "though" or "despite" for one that pushed against it.
- **Tipping beat fields** (`# TIPPING POINTS`, ids `tip-war`, `tip-collapse`, `tip-runaway`): Type, Headline (used in the results timeline), Timeline label, Future (where it leads), Text (what happened: how, why the machines did it, and the deaths, plainly), **Prevented** (v5.6: lines like openers, `- flag r3-drones/A | It might have been. In {year} you…`, ending `- default`; the engine shows the one tied to the player's most recent earlier decision, never the tipping decision itself), and optional `### Version` sub-items with a **When** and a Text, so the beat agrees with the Future story that follows (tip-war's illness version matches The Last War's Story 2). A Choice with a Tipping point also has **Tipping why** (v5.6): with `{year}`, what this decision did, why it was the last straw, and what each of the other two Choices would have done. `story-parser.js` fails a tipping Choice without one.
- **Values lost at the tipping point** (SETTINGS, v5.6) and each Value's **After the tipping point** line: once the world tips, those Values stop counting and the results show them grayed out at where they stood just before, with that line.
- **Pressure cards** (SETTINGS, v5.9): `Pressure cards` lists them and `Pressure question by round` the round by which a held or leveled game must have met one. Write them for both slow worlds where they can appear (`pace not racing, pace not paced, pace not beyond`, with a `### Version leveled` where the text differs). An Event that can only happen in a slow world must also exclude, by flag, worlds where a lift or exemption is already decided (`not flag lifted, not flag exempt_medicine`…), because an Event's conditions are tested when it is planned, before the milestones in its stretch land.
- **Ahead** (milestones, v5.6): future-tense lines like Because (`- flag r2-assisted/A | Expected on time. The research AIs you let run on their own in {year}…`, ending `- default`), shown in the gauge's dropdown while the milestone is the next one expected.
- **Future fields:** Title, Tagline, Accent, Targets (in brackets for The Long Middle), Requires (conditions, flags allowed), Path (shown only in the review documents), **Grounded by** (lines `- r2-glasses/C | sentence`, `- ev-crash | sentence` or `- tip-war | sentence`), optional Added, then `### Story 1…n`, each with optional **When** (v5.3: the world it belongs to, like `not reached sota-asi`; a Future is shown only a story whose When holds), Image, Image description and Text, and optional **Ending** (v5.8: lines like openers, `- flag r7-archive/C | sentence`, ending `- default | sentence`; the one tied to the most recent decision becomes the story's last paragraph, so it can close on a decision the player made), optional **Drawn by** (v6.0: like Follows, `r3-drones 1, r6-arms/A 0.5, ev-nearwar 1.5`: the cards, Choices and Events that lead to this story; of the stories that fit the world, the one the game's path points to most, each entry × Continuity decay ^ rounds since, is told if it reaches SETTINGS → Story pull minimum, else one of the stories without Drawn by at random) and optional **World** (v6.0: lines like openers, usually conditions on the world's AI such as `reached sota-held`, ending `- default`; the one that fits replaces a Text paragraph that is just `{world}`, so one story reads truly whatever the pace). The Long Middle has 14 stories (v6.0): six for its worlds (Ana, Tomás, Kwame, Lena, Mei-ling, Farah) and eight drawn by storylines, each for every world (Walt, an American veteran, for the military; Priya, the lab race; Sofía, agents; Arjun, the planet; Kadri, AI in government; Dolores, work; Beatriz, information and surveillance; Joseph, medicine). A Future whose stories assume superintelligence must require it (`reached sota-asi`), or have a story for the other worlds.
- **Versions** (v5.4): a Scenario or Event can hold `### Version name` sub-items, each with a **When** (conditions, tested at the item's own date, like a Future story's) and any of Headline, Timeline label, Place, Summary, Story and Question. The first version whose When holds replaces those fields; the Choices never change. Use a version when one card must read truly in different worlds (superintelligence or not), instead of writing a second card.
- **No contradictions** (v5.4): a card's text may assume only what its conditions guarantee, and a Future's story must never contradict an explicit Choice the player made. When a story would, block it in Requires (`not flag r5-socialcredit/B`), give it a story with a When, or soften the claim so every path to it supports it. `check-story.js` keeps a table of known contradictions (CONTRADICTIONS) that must never happen, and `--audit` writes a worlds report to read for new ones.
- **History:** lines `- Date | Title | Line | URL | Strip label`.

## Checks (`node tools/check-story.js [games] [--audit]`)

1. **Problems**, **chronology**, **warnings** (lint), **Futures whose required flags aren't fully grounded**, **broken image paths**.
2. **Style** over every player-facing field (above), links reduced to their words first; non-`https://` links flagged.
3. **SCALE** table.
4. **Simulation** (100,000 random games played exactly as the game plays them): Future shares against the targets (The Long Middle ≤ 30%, the six v5 Futures 4–8%, none other above 15%) and a sampled "seeking reach" per Future; **pools** per round (min / average / max) and safety-net uses; **arcs** (tipping 5–8%, agents era 10–15%, near miss 5–10%); **Events** per game and each Event's share (15–50%); share of games ending with a Value at exactly zero; **steering** (share of each Future's games grounded by a decision from round 5); **flow** (AGI 90–100% and on time in 70–90%, v5.5: self-improvement on time, superintelligence in 2035, paced in 2040, held or leveled off, and the pace question asked by round 3 in at least 90% of games; superintelligence 70–92%, the last card offering the leading Future in at least 60% of games, at least 75% of cards on the player's storylines, at least 85% following a decision from the last two rounds, at most 5% below the round's scale) and the default lead-in share; the **"never" checks** (no card that assumes more AI than the world has at its date, no second slowdown shown, no round without a card, nothing out of date order, no card twice, no After humanity card before a tipping point or ordinary card after one, no Event after one, no Long Middle after one, every required flag shown in How we got here, a lead-in for every card after round 1, Requires always met, every Future grounded, no card about AI stalling without a slowdown shown before it, no How we got here without a decision from round 4 on, no unfilled `{year}`, no two cards in a row with the same question, no gap over 9 years, no Future without a story for its world, no capability curve still climbing after AI leveled off unless the player raced again, v5.4: the filled part of the graph never changing, every Event that demands a response answered by the next card, and none of the known contradictions); each Event's share (15–50%) except near-miss Events, which follow every near miss; alignment; card shares; replays (game 2 at least 90% new).
5. **Audit** (`--audit`): `docs/path-audit.md`, including **Story timelines**: 30 random games as one line each, to read as stories.

The exit code is 1 on any problem, chronology or style problem, ungrounded required flag, broken image or "never" violation.
