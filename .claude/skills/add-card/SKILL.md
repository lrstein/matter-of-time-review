## Adding a storylet card

1. Pick its round and date: the date must fall inside the round's years (SETTINGS → Round years: 2027 | 2028–2031 | 2032–2035 | 2036–2040 | 2041–2045 | 2046–2050). Use an id like `r5-name`, and put it under its first round's `# ROUND n` section (ids kept their v5 numbers when v5.1 went from 7 rounds to 6, so an id's number is no longer its round) (the section is only for editing; the `**Round:**` field is what counts, and can be a range like `5-6` if the date fits all of them).
2. Copy an existing card's fields: Headline, Timeline label, Date, Place, Round, Image (empty), Image description, Summary, Story, Question and three Choices (Text, Effects, As a result). Mark it `**New in vN:** yes` if Levi should review it as new.
3. **Where it sits in the story.** Give it `**Threads:**` (its storylines: information, surveillance, work, medicine, minds, race, planet, ai-rule, autonomy), `**Scale:**` (local, national, bloc or world: how far its consequences reach; late rounds want bloc or world), and `**Follows:**` (the earlier cards, Choices and Events that lead to it, with strengths: `r3-policing 1, r3-policing/A 0.5, ev-heat 0.5`; a card id means any Choice on that card). The scratch script that generated v5.1's Follows gave each card its storylines' cards from the round before at 1 and two rounds before at 0.5, plus the Choices that lead to it most directly. **When it can appear.** If its subject must have come up before (headsets, uploading, the courts), require one of the cards that introduce it: `any flag r2-glasses, r3-worlds`. If it assumes a level of AI, require it: `capability >= AGI`, `reached sota-robots`, `reached sota-asi` (or `not reached sota-asi` for a slow-AI card). Leave out `**Appears when:**` and it can appear in any game in its rounds. Other conditions when the card needs a context: `any flag r5-moratorium/A, r5-moratorium/B`, `flag agents_era`, `unity <= 0`, `not flag pause`. Every condition must hold.
4. **Openers.** Write `- flag <card>/<Letter> | sentence` for earlier Choices and `- flag <card> | sentence` for earlier cards (any Choice) that this card follows, phrased so each reads well whether that decision came one round back or two, and so it says how the earlier, smaller story grew into this larger one. End with `- default | sentence`. The engine uses the opener tied to the most recent decision (a Choice's opener before its card's on a tie). Openers are wording only. `check-story.js` prints the share of lead-ins that fall back to a default (1% now): keep it low.
5. **The question** grows with the scale: local and national cards ask what a place or "your country" should do, bloc cards where "your country" should stand or how it should vote, world cards what "the world" should decide.
6. **Writing it** (v5.6, see docs/content.md). The Story is a New York Times news story: a lede with who, what, where and when, then specifics (numbers, institutions, terms explained once), the sourced history, reactions attributed by role, and what must be decided. Invented companies get real-sounding names, recorded in the Names table of docs/content.md. Each **As a result** covers only about a year after the card's date, at the card's scale, and never names a later year or settles what a later card decides. The Image description is a snapshot of ordinary life in the card's year, with no robots that don't exist yet.
7. **Choices.** Optional fields: `**Sets:** flagname` (named flags, read by other cards and Futures), `**Agents:** +1` (it hands decisions to AI), `**Tipping point:** control <= -2, unity <= -2 | war` (only on a few of the riskiest Choices, rounds 4 and 5; it needs a **Tipping why** too: what this decision did, why it was the last straw and what the other two Choices would have done, with `{year}`).
8. Add Grounded by lines on any Future the Choices lead to (`- r5-name/A | sentence`). If a Choice sets a flag a Future requires, it **must** be in that Future's Grounded by. Since v5.2 a Future is given only for a collection of decisions (two or more, one from round 4 on, or one from the last two rounds), so a late card should ground the Futures its Choices really lead to. Write each sentence as one contribution among several, never "the template for everything that followed". A milestone the card assumes may want a Because line for its Choices (`- flag r5-name/A | … in {year} …`).
9. Run `node tools/check-story.js` until clean, look at the card's share, the Flow section and the story timelines in `docs/path-audit.md` (`--audit`), and run the play-test.
